Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

These are general explanations for the prototype. Final terms depend on verified company policies and applicable requirements.

Does submitting a loan application guarantee approval?

No. An application is reviewed, and any decision depends on the relevant eligibility assessment and verification process.

What are the repayment terms?

Any applicable interest, fees, instalment amount and repayment dates must be disclosed and agreed in the actual loan agreement before disbursement.

Can I invest in LENDVESTRA today?

This prototype only collects demonstration enquiries. No investment subscription, deposit or payment is enabled. Official terms and permissions must be verified before any opportunity is offered.

Is funding loans the same as investing in the company?

No. Investing in the company and funding loans to borrowers are different activities with different rights, risks and regulatory considerations.

How is my information protected?

In production, the platform should use secure authentication, server-side validation, database access policies and appropriate retention controls. This standalone preview does not transmit or securely store submitted information.

How do I contact support?

Use the contact enquiry form in this preview. Official email and phone details will be added after they are provided and verified.
