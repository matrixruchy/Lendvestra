GOOD TO KNOW

Does submitting a loan application mean it is approved? ＋

No. An application is reviewed first. Approval depends on eligibility, assessment and applicable terms.

Can I invest directly in LENDVESTRA? ＋

You can submit an enquiry to request information. Any opportunity, terms and applicable permissions must be verified before an offer is made.

Is company investment the same as funding borrower loans? ＋

No. These are different activities with different structures and risks. The platform keeps these enquiry pathways separate.

Which currency does LENDVESTRA use? ＋

The current project is being designed around USD-denominated amounts. Applicable currency, fees and availability must be confirmed in the relevant service terms.
